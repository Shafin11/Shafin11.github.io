Research

Two pieces of work I'm proudest of, with the results that came out of them.

RNA-seq expression CRISPR dependency

Undergraduate thesis · BRAC University

Multimodal Bridge Framework for Gene Regulatory Network Discovery

Most methods guess gene relationships from expression data alone. My framework uses two encoders to bring RNA-seq expression and CRISPR knockout dependency data into one shared 128-dimensional space, then reads tissue-specific regulatory links from it.

  • Dual-encoder representation learning
  • Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD) alignment with diversity-preserving regularisation
  • Links scored with GENIE3, Spearman correlation and embedding similarity
Read the thesis on GitHub

Higher is better

Next-hour PM2.5 on 84,156 test hours from 12 Beijing stations. Errors in µg/m³.

Team research project · BRAC University

Next-Hour Urban PM2.5 Forecasting

Using the Beijing Multi-Site Air Quality Dataset (hourly, 2013–2017), we predicted the next hour's PM2.5 from pollutant and weather readings, and asked a simple question: do complex models actually beat simple ones?

  • Merged 12 stations, filled gaps and built time features
  • Time-ordered train/test split to avoid leakage, plus rolling validation over 2014–2016
  • Linear Regression won with R² ≈ 0.95 and stayed stable across all three validation years
See the paper and notebook
